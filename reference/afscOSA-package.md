# afscOSA: Calculate and Visualize Fits to AFSC Compositional Stock Assessment Data

One step ahead (OSA) residual diagnostic plots for composition data fit
in fisheries stock assessments at the Alaska Fisheries Science Center
(AFSC). For each data set (fleet) the output plots the fit aggregated
across years, a Q-Q plot of OSA residuals, bubble plots for OSA and
Pearson residuals. OSA residuals will be standard normal under a
correctly specified model and so statistics are also provided to give
context to the fit and guide understanding of causes of misfit. See
Stewart and Monnahan (2025) for information on background and
interpretation.
